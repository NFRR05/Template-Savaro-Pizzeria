Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Savaro Pizzeria, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. When you interact with our website, make a reservation, or order online, we may collect the following details:

  • Personal Identification Details: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Reservation & Order Data: Date, time, party size, dietary preferences, and special requests.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction logs via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing table reservations and online dining orders.
  • Communicating booking confirmations, updates, or customer support responses.
  • Improving user experience, website performance, and seasonal menu offerings.
  • Sending promotional emails or newsletters (only if explicitly opted-in).

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies and Google Analytics to monitor web traffic, measure engagement, and provide a seamless navigation experience. You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time without restricting your core browsing experience.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We implement industry-standard SSL encryption and security protocols to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR), you retain full control over your personal data. You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your information stored by Savaro Pizzeria at any time.
